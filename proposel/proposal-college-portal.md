# College Portal Proposal

## Executive Summary
This proposal describes a College Portal tailored to higher-education needs: course registration, semester planning, faculty workflows, research/project management, and student services. The portal streamlines administrative tasks and provides a scalable platform for academic services.

## Objectives
- Simplify course registration and timetable management
- Support faculty with course materials, grading, and research collaboration
- Provide students with degree planning and scholarship information
- Expose analytics for administrators on enrollment and performance

## Scope
- Course catalog, registration, and waitlists
- Faculty profiles, course sites, and research/project pages
- Student degree audit and progress tracking
- Scholarship and financial aid information
- Events, notices, and campus announcements

## Key Features
- Self-service course registration with conflict detection
- Faculty tools for assignment distribution, grading, and feedback
- Degree progress dashboard and elective suggestions
- Research project pages and collaboration spaces
- Admin reporting on enrollment, retention, and grades

## Technical Approach
- Node.js/Express backend with Postgres for relational data
- Modular services for registration, academics, and reporting
- Authentication with university SSO integration (optional)

## Implementation Plan & Timeline
- Phase 1 (3 weeks): Data model, course catalog, and authentication
- Phase 2 (4 weeks): Registration, faculty tools, and gradebook
- Phase 3 (2 weeks): Degree audit, analytics, and reporting
- Phase 4 (1 week): Testing and deployment

## Data & Security
- SSO integration, RBAC, and encrypted connections (TLS)
- Data retention policies for academic records

## Estimated Budget
- Development: 8–10 developer-weeks
- Hosting and storage scaled by student population

## Conclusion
The College Portal supports scalable academic operations, improves student services, and empowers faculty and administrators with modern tools.
