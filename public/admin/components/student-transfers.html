Student Transfers

Request student transfers and review incoming requests.

Transfer process
  • The receiving institution reviews and accepts or rejects each request.
  • On acceptance, the student's existing login is retained and the receiving school assigns a class.
  • Parent/guardian profiles and credentials are copied to destination logins; source logins remain for any siblings.
  • Previous academic history stays preserved in the transfer archive.
