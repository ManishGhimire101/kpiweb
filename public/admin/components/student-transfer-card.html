What happens after you send the request?
  • The receiving institution reviews the student's profile and parent/guardian details.
  • The student account and password are retained when the request is accepted.
  • Academic records remain preserved with the transfer history from the sending institution.
  • The student is placed into a class by the receiving institution after acceptance.
