From nothing to a working school in three steps.

No installation and no setup project. A school is usable the same day it is registered.

  1. 01

    Register the school

    Pick a name and an address. The address is checked live, and the first administrator account is created for you.

  2. 02

    Add people

    Create classes, add teachers, admit students together with their parents. Everyone gets a sign-in.

  3. 03

    Start teaching

    Homework, resources and attendance are ready on day one, and the school website is already live.
