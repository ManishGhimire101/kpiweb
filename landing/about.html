Software that stays out of the way.

Shikshya means education. It is a small, focused platform for schools that want order without the noise.

Most school software tries to be everything: accounting, transport, chat, social feeds. Teachers end up learning a tool instead of teaching a class.

Shikshya starts from the other end. A school needs to hand out work, collect it, record who came, and keep families informed. It also needs a public face. We built exactly that, and gave each school its own address so it feels like theirs.

Parents hear from teachers, and that is where it stops. Messages go one way on purpose, so a note home stays a note and not the start of a long argument.

Four things we will not trade away.

Each school stands alone

A school's people, files and settings are bound to its own address. Signing in at one school can never reach another.

Small on purpose

Every screen does one job. If a feature does not help the daily loop of teaching, it is not here.

Calm communication

Teachers can raise a concern with a parent. Parents read it. No threads, no reply storms, no notifications to chase.

Ready on day one

A new school gets a working portal and a finished website immediately, with its own name, logo and colours.

Who gets what.

Platform ownerRegisters schools, chooses addresses, sets logo and colours, creates the first administrator, pauses or reactivates a school.
School adminCreates classes, adds teachers, admits students with parents, resets passwords, manages the public website.
TeacherAssigns and grades homework, shares class resources, takes attendance, sends notes to parents.
StudentSees homework, uploads answers, reads grades and feedback, opens resources, checks attendance.
ParentSees each child's homework and attendance and reads teacher messages.

One owner, many schools.

The platform owner has a separate address and a separate sign-in, and manages every school from one list.
